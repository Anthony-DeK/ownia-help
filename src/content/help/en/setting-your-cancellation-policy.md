A clear cancellation policy protects your revenue when plans change while still giving guests confidence to book directly with you instead of a marketplace.

## Where to set it

Open a property from **Properties**, then open the **Online Booking** tile to reach **Online Booking Settings**. Inside, you'll find the **Cancellation policy** section.

## How tiers work

Your cancellation policy is made up of tiered rules based on how many days before check-in a guest cancels. For each tier, you set:

- **Days before check-in**
- **Refund percentage**

For example, you might offer a full refund for cancellations 14+ days out, a 50% refund between 7 and 13 days out, and no refund inside 7 days. You can add as many tiers as you need, and they apply automatically — you don't have to manually approve each cancellation for it to be enforced correctly.

## What guests see

Your cancellation policy is shown to guests on your booking page before they pay, so there are no surprises later. If a guest cancels through their booking confirmation, the refund amount is calculated automatically against the tier that applies on the day they cancel.

## Cancellation policy vs. security deposit

These are two separate settings that are easy to conflate:

- **Cancellation policy** governs what a guest is refunded if *they* cancel before arriving.
- **[Security deposit](/en/how-security-deposits-work/)** is a separate hold placed on the guest's card right before check-out, used to cover damage or issues found during the stay.

Configuring one does not affect the other — most hosts set up both.
