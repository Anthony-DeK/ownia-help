Airbnb and Booking.com typically charge hosts somewhere between 3% and 18% per booking depending on the platform and listing type, on top of what they charge guests in service fees. Ownia charges a flat **5% commission per booking, on every plan, with no listing fees** — and unlike a marketplace, you also keep the guest relationship: their email, their booking history, and the ability to bring them back for a repeat stay without paying a marketplace a second time.

None of this means you should delete your Airbnb or Booking.com listings — those platforms are still valuable for discovery. The goal is to give guests who already know about you (past guests, referrals, your own audience) a better reason to book directly instead of defaulting to a marketplace out of habit.

## Make your booking page easy to find and use

Your Ownia Web Store is your direct-booking website. A few things make it more effective:

- [Connect a custom domain](/en/setting-up-a-custom-domain/) so the link is memorable and looks professional, not like a generic third-party URL.
- Keep your [calendar synced](/en/syncing-calendars-via-ical/) so availability shown on your booking page is always accurate — nothing undermines trust in a direct-booking site like a stale calendar.
- Fill out your property description, photos, and amenities fully. Guests comparing your direct page to your Airbnb listing will notice if one looks less complete than the other.

## Where to send guests

A few channels consistently work well for driving direct bookings:

- **Past guests** — after a stay, invite guests to book directly next time, ideally with a small incentive (a discount code, a free late checkout) that costs you less than the commission you'd otherwise pay a marketplace.
- **Your own website or social bio** — if you have any web presence, link straight to your Web Store.
- **QR codes in the property itself** — a card near the welcome book pointing returning or referred guests to book directly next time.
- **Search engines** — a custom domain with real content (descriptions, photos, and this kind of help content) is what search engines and AI assistants actually index; a marketplace listing is not something you control or that links back to you.

## Give guests a reason, not just a link

Guests default to marketplaces mostly out of habit and trust, not loyalty. The way to shift that is to make booking directly the obviously better option — typically a small price advantage (since you're not passing along the guest-side service fee marketplaces add), faster direct communication with you, and the same [secure Stripe payment](/en/connecting-stripe-to-accept-payments/) and [cancellation protection](/en/setting-your-cancellation-policy/) they'd expect from a marketplace.
